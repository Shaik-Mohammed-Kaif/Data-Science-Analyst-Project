# 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most important business problems for
subscription-based companies.

When customers stop using a company's services, the business can lose:

- 💰 Recurring revenue
- 👥 Customers
- 📈 Customer Lifetime Value
- 🤝 Long-term customer relationships
- 📊 Business growth opportunities

This project presents an **end-to-end Customer Churn Analytics and
Prediction system** designed to understand customer behavior, identify
churn patterns, analyze revenue risk, and predict customers who are likely
to churn.

The project combines:

**Python + Pandas + NumPy + SQL + SQLite + Machine Learning + Power BI + Streamlit**

The complete workflow moves from raw customer data to business-ready
insights and predictive analytics.

---

# 🏢 Business Context

This project represents a **Telecom / Subscription-Based Customer
Business**.

Customers use different telecom services such as:

- 📱 Phone Services
- 🌐 Internet Services
- 📺 Streaming Services
- 🔐 Online Security
- ☁️ Online Backup
- 🛡️ Device Protection
- 🛠️ Premium Technical Support

Customers may discontinue their services because of:

- High monthly charges
- Short tenure
- Contract type
- Service problems
- Payment issues
- Poor customer experience
- Lack of support
- Limited service usage
- Customer dissatisfaction

The business wants to identify **why customers leave, which customers
are at risk, and how the company can reduce churn.**

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer churn and wants to understand the
underlying customer behavior.

The central business questions are:

> **Why are customers leaving?**

> **Which customer segments have higher churn?**

> **Which customers are at higher risk of leaving?**

> **How much revenue is potentially at risk?**

The analysis transforms raw customer data into actionable business
intelligence.

The major objectives of this project are:
